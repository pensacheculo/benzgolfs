How It Works

How the program works

You are placed on a starting hole, given the exact skills to earn, and you move to the next hole when you show them on video.

01 / PLACE

Get placed

Send two swings. Jakob's placement breakdown gives you your starting hole, your one biggest fault, and your first drill block.

02 / TRAIN

Run your drills

Each hole has a drill plan you can run at the range or in the garage. 15–20 minutes, 2–3x a week. No launch monitor needed.

03 / TEST

Film your test

When you are ready, film your hole test using the same two angles and send it in for assessment.

04 / ADVANCE

Move to the next hole

Pass the gates and you advance. New badge, new drills, next hole. If you do not pass, you get the exact reason and what to fix.

The Levels

Five holes. Played in order.

Faults have an order. Distance is useless on a broken strike, and a strike is useless on a broken setup. The Drive fixes your swing in that order.

B Hole 1

Red Tee: The Foundation

Grip, setup, posture, alignment. +

Goal: a repeatable setup. It is the fastest way to drop shots, and every hole after this one depends on it.

Neutral grip held through 10 straight swings (no re-grip at the top)
Posture checkpoint: spine angle set and held at address, face-on & down-the-line
Alignment: feet/hips/shoulders matched to target line with a club down
Consistent ball position for wedge, mid-iron, and driver
Typical time: 2–4 weeksTest: setup video, 3 clubsBadge: Red dot
B Hole 2

Gold Tee: The Strike

Contact and low point control. +

Goal: ball first, ground second, every time. Controlling your low point removes thin and fat shots.

7/10 ball-first strikes with a mid-iron (towel or line drill on video)
Divot starts in front of the ball, not behind it
Weight on the lead side at impact, no hanging back
Centered face contact 6/10 (foot-spray or impact tape)
Typical time: 4–8 weeksTest: 10-ball strike testBadge: Gold dot
B Hole 3

White Tee: The Flight

Ball flight control. +

Goal: the ball starts where you aim it and curves the way you chose. You learn to control face and path.

7/10 shots starting on your intended line
One reliable stock shape (draw or fade) you can call before you swing
Big miss reduced: no more than 1 in 10 with the old curve
Trajectory control: show a stock, a lower, and a higher flight with one club
Typical time: 6–10 weeksTest: called-shot video testBadge: White dot
B Hole 4

Blue Tee: The Engine

Speed and distance without losing your strike. +

Goal: more distance through better sequencing, not harder swinging. Ground force, rotation, and a reliable driver.

Proper sequence on video: hips lead, chest follows, arms last
Driver carry up a measurable step from your Level 3 baseline
Speed session protocol done 2x/week for four weeks
Keep it: 7/10 fairway-window drives at your new speed
Typical time: 8–12 weeksTest: driver session videoBadge: Blue dot
B Hole 5

The Tips: The Scorer

On-course scoring. The final hole. +

Goal: take the swing to the course. Scoring shots, smart misses, and a pre-shot routine that holds up under pressure.

Wedge distance control: three stock numbers you can hit on demand
On-course video: routine + commitment on 9 documented holes
Miss management: your bad shot is playable (show the pattern)
Beat your entry handicap by the target you and Jakob set
Typical time: ongoingTest: on-course assessmentBadge: Black dot, Tips graduate
Your Scorecard

You always know where you stand

Every member gets a scorecard: your current hole, the gates you have passed, and the next skill you need. Nothing vague.

When you pass a hole you get a badge: the B-dot in that hole's color, sized for an Instagram story. Post it and tag Benz Golfs.
