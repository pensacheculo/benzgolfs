FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I film my swing correctly?

Two angles: face-on (camera directly facing your chest, hip height) and down-the-line (camera behind you, looking down the target line, hip height). Normal speed is fine. I'll slow it down on my end. Full swing with a mid-iron or driver, whichever you're struggling with. You'll get a short guide with examples right after checkout.

How fast do I get my breakdown?

Within 72 hours of your videos landing, usually faster. On-course demo packages can take a couple extra days since I film your demo footage personally.

Am I good enough (or too good) for this?

If you swing a golf club, you qualify. I've reviewed swings from beginners to single-digit handicaps. The faults change, the process doesn't: find the one thing costing you the most, fix that first.

What do I get, exactly?

A personal video: your swing in slow motion with lines and angles drawn on it, a plain-English explanation of your 1–2 biggest faults, and specific drills to fix them. It's yours to keep and rewatch. This isn't a template; every video is recorded from scratch for your swing.

What if I'm not happy with my review?

Tell me. If your breakdown didn't give you something clear and useful to work on, I'll re-review your swing or refund you. Simple as that.

Do you offer ongoing coaching?

Yes, soon. The Drive is a monthly program with ongoing video assessments and direct access. It is not open yet; you can join the waitlist on the program page. For now, plenty of golfers send a re-check breakdown a few weeks after their first one to confirm the fix is sticking.
